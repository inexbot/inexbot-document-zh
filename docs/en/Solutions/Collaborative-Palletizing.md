# Collaborative Robot Palletizing Solution

## Product Introduction

The INEXBOT collaborative robot palletizing solution is a complete industry solution developed for the logistics, food, pharmaceutical and other industries. INEXBOT collaborative robots paired with a dedicated palletizing process package enable efficient and precise box stacking, suitable for a variety of product specifications and stacking patterns.

INEXBOT collaborative robots are known for high safety, high flexibility, and easy deployment. They can work alongside humans without safety fencing, greatly reducing space requirements and deployment costs.

## Product Advantages

### High Safety

Collaborative robots have built-in collision detection. In human-robot collaboration scenarios, if the robot collides with an operator, it immediately stops and retreats to ensure operator safety. The collision detection sensitivity can also be adjusted via parameters to suit different loads and scenarios.

### Flexible Deployment

Collaborative robots are compact and lightweight, and can be moved and installed by a single person. The INEXBOT dedicated palletizing process package provides a visual configuration interface, allowing quick adaptation to new product specifications without programming.

### Multiple Stacking Patterns

The INEXBOT palletizing process supports multiple stacking patterns, including:

- **Row/column mode**: standard row/column arrangement, supporting overall rotation angles (0°, 90°, 180°, -90°)
- **Interlocking**: adjacent layers staggered to improve stability
- **Square-ring mode**: inner and outer rings stacked alternately, suitable for specific packaging
- **Five-flower stack**: plum-blossom-shaped stacking, suitable for round objects
- **Custom**: supports user-defined stacking rules

### Convenient Simulation and Debugging

INEXBOT provides PC-side simulation software supporting offline programming and trajectory simulation. Engineers can complete trajectory planning, cycle time estimation, and collision detection on a PC, greatly reducing on-site debugging time.

### High Protection Rating

Collaborative robots can be optionally equipped with an IP68 protection rating version, suitable for harsh environments such as humid and dusty conditions.

## Q&A

**Q: What stacking patterns does the INEXBOT collaborative robot palletizing solution support?**

A: It supports five patterns: row/column mode, interlocking, square-ring mode, five-flower stack, and custom stacking rules. Each pattern supports overall rotation angle adjustment (0°, 90°, 180°, -90°).

**Q: Which industries is the collaborative robot palletizing solution suitable for?**

A: It is mainly suitable for logistics (parcel stacking), food (box palletizing), pharmaceutical (medicine box stacking), electronics (part loading) and other industries, with a typical load range of 1-20kg.

**Q: What advantages does the INEXBOT palletizing solution have over traditional industrial robot solutions?**

A: Core advantages include: high safety (human-robot collaboration without safety fencing), compact size (saving 30-50% space), flexible deployment (single-person transport and installation), and fast line changeover (specification switching in as little as 15 minutes).

**Q: What product changeover methods does the palletizing solution support?**

A: Product specifications can be directly modified through the teach pendant parameter configuration screen without reprogramming. Configuration items include pallet size, workpiece parameters, placement point height, entry point position, etc., allowing quick adaptation to products of different sizes.

**Q: What is the INEXBOT palletizing cycle time?**

A: The standard cycle time is about 600-1000 pieces per hour, depending on product specifications (size, weight), stacking height, and motion parameter configuration.

**Q: Does the collaborative robot require safety fencing?**

A: INEXBOT collaborative robots have collision detection and can work alongside humans without safety fencing under standard configuration. However, whether fencing is needed should be determined based on on-site risk assessment; safety fencing is still recommended for some heavy-load or high-speed scenarios.
