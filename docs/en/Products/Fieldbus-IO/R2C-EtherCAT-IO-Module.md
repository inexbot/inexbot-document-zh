## Q&A

**Q: What is the difference between the R2C and the R4C/R4D/R5B?**

A: The R2C is a compact IO module with the smallest dimensions (72×140mm). It provides only 16 DI + 16 DO (MOSFET output) and has no analog interfaces or encoder interface, making it suitable for space-constrained scenarios that only need digital input/output. The R4C/R4D are larger (122×200mm) and additionally provide 2 AI + 2 AO + encoder interface + CAN expansion. The R5B is the largest (116×210.9mm) and provides 20 DO (16 transistor + 4 relay) + analog + encoder.

**Q: Which EtherCAT functions does the R2C support?**

A: The R2C supports a 100Mbps bus rate and distributed clock (DC) functionality, with a minimum communication cycle of 200us. It can connect with INEXBOT controllers and master station devices that support the standard EtherCAT protocol.

**Q: Can the R2C be expanded via the bus?**

A: Yes. The R2C can be cascaded with other IO modules or master station devices via the EtherCAT bus to expand system scale.

**Q: How is the R2C installed?**

A: The R2C has a built-in standard DIN rail mounting slot and can be clipped directly onto a DIN rail for quick, easy installation and removal.

**Q: What are the characteristics of the R2C's MOSFET output?**

A: The R2C uses MOSFETs as the digital output elements, offering fast response, long service life, and no mechanical wear — ideal for high-frequency switching industrial applications.

**Q: What is the operating temperature range of the R2C?**

A: The R2C operating temperature range is 0~60°C, with an even wider storage temperature range, making it suitable for industrial site deployment.
