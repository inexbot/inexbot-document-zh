## Product Advantages

### 1. Powerful Performance, HD Interaction

Powered by a Cortex-A7 dual-core 1.2GHz ARM processor paired with an 8-inch 800×600 HD TFT display, it delivers smooth graphics rendering and >98% visibility under strong light. The LCD has a service life of up to 20,000 hours, meeting the demands of long-term stable operation in industrial environments.

### 2. Precise Touch, Industrial Adaptability

Featuring a 4-wire industrial resistive touchscreen with strong anti-interference capability, it supports precise operation even with industrial gloves. A stylus is included as standard to cover a wide range of industrial operation scenarios.

### 3. Three-Stage Enable, Safety Redundancy

A three-position selector switch (manual/stop/auto), combined with a three-stage dual-contact enable switch (not activated/enabled/panic), achieves a misoperation rate of <0.1%, providing comprehensive operational safety.

### 4. Dual-Contact E-Stop, Flexible Integration

The emergency stop switch features a dual-contact twist-to-unlock design and is available in 2-wire (one normally-closed set) or 4-wire (two normally-closed sets) configurations, supporting communication or I/O connection.

### 5. Precise Fine Adjustment, Flexible Definition

Equipped with an AB-phase pulse-signal electronic handwheel (20 pulses/revolution) supporting mouse/fine-adjust mode switching. 21 membrane keys + 4 customizable LED indicators satisfy personalized operation requirements.

### 6. High Durability, Low Power Consumption

Rated power consumption < 8W; the cable withstands 50,000 cycles of 60° swing without complete breakage. Passes 6-face drop testing from 1 meter with no casing cracks and full functionality intact.

### 7. Flexible Deployment, Soft Integration

Ethernet communication bus + USB HOST 2.0 interface as standard, with wiring adapter box and aviation connector — plug-and-play, easy deployment.

### 8. Extreme Environments, Handled with Ease

Operating temperature -20℃ ~ 70℃, storage temperature -20℃ ~ 85℃, ambient humidity 10~90%RH (non-condensing), IP65 protection rating (display front panel), 10~25Hz vibration resistance (2G/10 min in X/Z directions).
