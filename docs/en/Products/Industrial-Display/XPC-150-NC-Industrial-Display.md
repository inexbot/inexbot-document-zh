# Industrial Display XPC-150-NC

## Product Introduction

The XPC-150-NC is a 15-inch high-performance touch all-in-one developed by INEXBOT for demanding industrial environments. It features a rugged all-aluminum housing with a compact fanless heat-dissipation architecture, and its front panel meets the IP65 industrial protection standard. The hardware platform flexibly adapts Intel® Core™ processors (up to 12th-gen i7), and comes with a 10-point capacitive touch HD large screen, perfectly combining outstanding computing power with precise interaction.

The device fully supports both Windows and Linux ecosystems and provides a rich set of industrial communication interfaces, making it an ideal human-machine interface (HMI) and edge computing terminal for core scenarios such as intelligent manufacturing, equipment control, data acquisition, and edge computing.

## Product Advantages

### Industrial Large Screen, Dynamic Interaction

A 15-inch industrial-grade LCD display with high color fidelity remains clearly visible even under strong workshop lighting. Projected capacitive touch technology supports 10-point concurrent touch with responsive performance, and works perfectly with wet hands and light gloves. The front panel meets the IP65 dustproof and waterproof standard, resisting dust, water droplets, and oil contamination found in industrial environments.

### Powerful Performance, Dual Systems

Supports up to 64GB DDR4 dual-channel memory, with M.2 NVMe high-speed SSD and 2.5-inch SATA dual storage expansion, easily handling complex vision algorithms and multi-task concurrent processing. Fully compatible with Windows 10/11 and mainstream Linux operating systems, seamlessly integrating with various host computer software, SCADA systems, and industrial control platforms.

### Flexible Expansion, Reliable Operation

Comes standard with 2 COM ports (RS232/RS485 selectable), expandable to up to 6, easily connecting PLCs, sensors, and various industrial instruments. Integrated 2×USB 3.0, 2×USB 2.0, 1×HDMI HD output, audio interface, and 4 isolated GPIO channels (digital input/output), meeting complex peripheral connection needs.

The all-aluminum housing with a compact fanless cooling design eliminates dust intake and fan mechanical failure, achieving zero-noise, maintenance-free operation. It supports automatic power-on at startup and, together with the one-touch CMOS clear button, greatly simplifies on-site batch deployment and later maintenance processes.
